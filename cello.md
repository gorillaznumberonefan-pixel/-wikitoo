# Cello
An instrument that usually has four strings and is in the violin family.
## Description
The violoncello, also more commonly known as the cello, is an instrument in the violin family. It is smaller than a bass, but bigger than a viola. the strings are usually tuned to C2, G2, D3, and A3.
